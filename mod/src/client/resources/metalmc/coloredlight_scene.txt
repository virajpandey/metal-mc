# Colored block light's test gallery (docs/lighting-design.md, "Colored block light"), as vanilla commands: the bench
# tour runs them (-PbenchTour=coloredlight, metalmc.bench.ColoredLightTour), tools/litflow.swift applies them offline
# (LITFLOW_CL=1, ColoredLightOffline.swift: fill and setblock only, absolute coordinates).
#
# A stone floor floating at y 199 (x -26..26, z 12..66). In front (z 14-38) a mixing area: a torch, a soul torch and a
# redstone torch 6 blocks apart, magma, candles. Behind it 18 cells, 7 x 7 inside, walled 4 high with stone bricks (1-block
# walls: light mustn't leak through them, only over the top), each with one light: the first row open toward the mixing
# area.
fill -26 199 12 26 199 66 minecraft:smooth_stone
fill -26 200 12 26 206 66 minecraft:air
fill -24 200 48 24 203 48 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill -24 200 56 24 203 56 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill -24 200 64 24 203 64 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill -24 200 40 -24 203 64 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill -16 200 40 -16 203 64 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill -8 200 40 -8 203 64 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill 0 200 40 0 203 64 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill 8 200 40 8 203 64 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill 16 200 40 16 203 64 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill 24 200 40 24 203 64 minecraft:stone_bricks
# Row 1 (z 44, open toward the mixing area): torch, soul torch, redstone torch, lantern, soul lantern, campfire.
setblock -20 200 44 minecraft:torch
setblock -12 200 44 minecraft:soul_torch
setblock -4 200 44 minecraft:redstone_torch
setblock 4 200 44 minecraft:lantern
setblock 12 200 44 minecraft:soul_lantern
setblock 20 200 44 minecraft:campfire[lit=true]
# Row 2 (z 52): glowstone, sea lantern, end rod, the three froglights.
setblock -20 200 52 minecraft:glowstone

# Row 3 (z 60): shroomlight, jack o'lantern, crying obsidian, an amethyst cluster, glow lichen, a lava pool (on a floor of
# its own: the platform floats).
setblock -20 200 60 minecraft:shroomlight
setblock -12 200 60 minecraft:jack_o_lantern[facing=north]
setblock -4 200 60 minecraft:crying_obsidian
setblock 4 200 60 minecraft:amethyst_cluster[facing=up]
setblock 12 200 60 minecraft:glow_lichen[down=true]
fill 19 198 59 21 198 61 minecraft:smooth_stone
fill 19 199 59 21 199 61 minecraft:lava
